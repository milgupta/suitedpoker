A 4-bet makes the pot enormous and the decision short. At a hundred big blinds, facing a 4-bet is often close to a shove-or-fold question. Calling leaves you playing a huge pot with a capped range, and the hands that genuinely want to call are mostly the hands that also wanted to put the rest in. If you feel yourself searching for a “safe” flat with a medium hand, you are usually manufacturing a reason to lose a bigger pot later.

## Who 4-bet matters more than almost anywhere else

An under-the-gun 4-bet is one of the strongest ranges in six-max cash. A button 4-bet is still strong, but it is wider — more bluffs, more speculative value, more hands that chose aggression over a flat. Against under the gun you continue with a tiny premium band. Against the button you can keep a little more queens and ace-king in mixed frequencies without inventing a fantasy.

If two charts give the same answer for those two seats, one of them is lying about position. The product used to ship that lie across every 4-bet node. The pairings you drill here were rewritten so the 4-bettor’s seat actually changes the answer.

## Calling a 4-bet is rare on purpose

Flatting a 4-bet out of position with a hand that is not happy stacking off is how beginners light big blinds on fire without noticing the leak in their session graph. Prefer shoving the hands that want stacks in the middle, and folding the rest. The small calling band that remains — usually some ace-king and the occasional queen — exists to keep your range from being pure shove-or-fold against observant opponents, not because flatting is the safer emotional choice.

Stack depth matters here more than in any earlier lesson. At a hundred big blinds the math is already compressed. Shallower, and the continue band shrinks further. Deeper, and more flatting can appear — but this product trains the hundred-big-blind tree, so learn that tree first.

## Practice the biggest pot

These drills are sparse by design — 4-bet pots are rare at the table and rare in the training set. When one shows up, treat it as high-stakes homework. One soft call here costs more expected value than twenty routine opens, which is exactly why the earlier modules spent so long teaching you to fold the dominated stuff before the pot got this large.
